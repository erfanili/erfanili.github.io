Ph.D. student, Electrical & Computer Engineering, Purdue University
Advised by Prof. Qiang Qiu · Ph.D. in Theoretical Physics

I work on post-training generative models with reinforcement learning: designing rewards that capture what we actually want from a model, and optimizing toward them. My recent work fine-tunes diffusion models with RL using gradient-based influence rewards (ECCV 2026), so that the synthetic data they generate measurably helps downstream classifiers. I am now carrying the same questions (reward design, credit assignment, reward hacking) over to RL post-training of LLMs.

I also study how attention works inside generative models, including training-free attention control for text-to-image alignment (CVPR 2025, co-first author). Before machine learning, I earned a Ph.D. in theoretical physics.

Research

RL post-training · Diffusion

Learned rewards for useful synthetic data

Class-Contrastive Influence scores how much a generated sample helps a downstream classifier. Used as an RL reward, it fine-tunes diffusion models to produce data that improves few-shot medical classification.

RL post-training · LLMs (in progress)

RL for multi-hop search agents (in progress)

GRPO post-training of Qwen2.5-3B search agents; matches Search-R1-3B on multi-hop QA with 0.75% of its training prompts. Studying whether agents learn real multi-hop retrieval or shortcuts, and how reward design changes that.

Attention in generative models

Attention control for text-to-image alignment

Cross-attention in diffusion models ignores word relations that the text encoder's self-attention already captures. Steering cross-attention maps toward that structure, with no training, fixes attribute binding and missing objects.
