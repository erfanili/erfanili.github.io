Quantized arithmetic

In quantized models, integer operations are not truly low-bit arithmetic: accumulations happen in higher precision (usually int32). The scale and zero-point conversions make the math approximate, but safe. It's a subtle design that preserves speed without fully abandoning numerical stability.
